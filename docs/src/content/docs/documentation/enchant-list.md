## Enchantments

### Soulbound
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.soulbound`

**Description**:
Keep the item in your inventory after death.

### Replanting
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.replanting`

**Description**:
Replants broken crops using seeds in your inventory or from the loot of the crop if no seeds are available in players inventory.

### Irrigation
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.irrigation`

**Description**:
Right-clicking farmland with an enchanted hoe rehydrates nearby dry farmland in a 3x3 area. Sneaking keeps the effect single-target for precision farm work.

### Arborist
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.arborist`

**Description**:
Using an enchanted axe to strip a log also strips up to a short vertical segment of matching trunk above it. Sneaking disables the extra spread for precision work.

### Trailblazer
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.trailblazer`

**Description**:
Using an enchanted shovel to flatten a block also spreads the dirt path to nearby flattenable blocks in a 3x3 area. Sneaking disables the spread for precision work.

### Furrow
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.furrow`

**Description**:
Harvests mature crops in a 3x3 area when using a hoe. Sneaking temporarily disables the bulk harvest so players can harvest a single crop precisely.

### Telepathy
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.telepathy`

**Description**:
Teleports dropped items to players location and makes them immediately pickuppable.

### Executioner
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.executioner`

**Description**:
Items enchanted with executioner will deal more damage to entities under specific health threshold.

### Beheading
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.beheading`

**Description**:
Adds a chance to drop the head of the entity killed (if entity has a head item available).

### Smelting
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.smelting`

**Description**:
Automatically smelts drops from mined blocks.

### Stonewake
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.stonewake`

**Description**:
Breaking an ore with an enchanted pickaxe grants a brief burst of haste, helping the next few mining actions without adding any passive tick cost.

### Airbag
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.airbag`

**Description**:
Reduces damage from hitting a wall while flying with elytra.

### Homecoming
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.homecoming`

**Description**:
When totem of undying is activated, player is teleported to their spawn point. If spawn point is not set, player is teleported to world spawn.

### Volley
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.volley`

**Description**:
Shoots additional arrows when shooting a bow. Amount of arrows and their spread is configurable.

### Ward
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.ward`

**Description**:
Automatically blocks hits, but triggers a configurable cooldown every time it does. It triggers a cooldown for
the enchanted items cooldown group, so if you make this enchantment support more than just 1 item, you're going to have
to handle applying the cooldown group to them yourself.

### Tenderstep
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.tenderstep`

**Description**:
Boots enchanted with Tenderstep prevent the wearer from trampling farmland, and can optionally protect turtle eggs too.

### Freshcatch
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.freshcatch`

**Description**:
Catching edible fish with an enchanted rod restores a little hunger and saturation, making long fishing sessions more sustainable.

### Starwish
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.starwish`

**Description**:
Using an enchanted spyglass to gaze into the night sky grants a brief burst of night vision and luck, with a tiny shooting-star flourish as an easter egg reward.

### Waysong
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.waysong`

**Description**:
Blowing an enchanted goat horn gives the user and nearby players a short travel speed boost, turning an overlooked utility item into a fun expedition signal.

### Delvesense
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.delvesense`

**Description**:
Right-clicking an enchanted compass underground grants a brief burst of night vision and outlines nearby hostile mobs, giving cave expeditions a low-overhead scouting tool.

### Tideshell
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchantment.tideshell`

**Description**:
Using an enchanted nautilus shell while touching water grants a short burst of water breathing and dolphin's grace, turning a neglected treasure item into an emergency dive tool.

## Curses

### Curse of Panic
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchant.panic_curse`

**Description**:
When player takes damage, there is a chance that their hotbar items will be scrambled.

### Curse of Vampirism
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchant.vampirism_curse`

**Description**:
Player is set on fire when exposed to direct sunlight.

### Curse of Insomnia
**Translation key**: `enchadd.enchant.insomnia_curse`

**Description**:
Player will not count as sleeping for the purpose of skipping the night/thunderstorm.
